A Simplified System for Plain Needlework. 


All former editions of “ Dress Cutting, Drafting, and French 
Pattern Modelling,” are out of date. 


T h e materials required for Drafting, Measuring, 
Cutting Out, and Fitting, are: 


A Tailor’s Square, or Eule. 


A Tape Measure. 


Tailor’s Chalk. 


A piece of cloth, on which to practise drafting 
(the top of an old card table is an excellent 
substitute). 


A sheet of brown paper, on which to draft the 
pattern, when perfect in drawing on the cloth. 


About 1-J- yards of calico, at 2Jd. per yard, or soft 
brown holland (as used by dressmakers), in which 
to cut out the pattern of the bodice, and one sleeve, 
for trying on. 


Scissors. 


Pins, &c. 


It will be seen that no charts are needed; the 
implements and materials required are of the 
simplest kinds.
