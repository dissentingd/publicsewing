# Apron riding skirt

**Source:** Mitchell, *Ladies' Tailor-Made Garments* (1921), "Ladies Apron Riding Skirt," Parts I and II, book pp. 118–121 (PDF pp. 122–125), corpus `newstandardworko00mitc`. US public domain.

**Block:** FreeSewing's Sarah supplies body measurements. The two aprons are drawn from Mitchell's point coordinates rather than Sarah's skirt outlines. Part I is the wide asymmetric front drape; Part II is the narrower back drape with a curved cutaway. The two straps are separate flat pieces.

**Threadloop:** no free riding skirt ("riding skirt" finds hoods and corsets). The nearest, "culottes", has 18 free patterns, all divided skirts rather than an apron over breeches; nothing listed says it's drafted from measurements.

**Status:** draft. Not sewn.

## What Mitchell states and what the plates show

The draft is for a 26½-inch waist, 40-inch hip, 37-inch front length and 38-inch back length. Part I's construction runs from centre front A down 39 inches to its curved hem; it extends 30¼ inches to one side of the lower front and 22 inches to the other. Its upper side has a deep shaped cutaway reaching point 17, where the long leg strap attaches. The short button tab attaches above point 15.

Part II is separately drafted from the centre back. Its left hem extends 14¼ inches from point 28 and its lower right reaches 5½ inches beyond point 29. The plate has three back dart shapes and a curved cutaway from point 32 toward the line through 36 and 37. Mitchell puts a seam opening about 10–11 inches below point 38. A long 1½-inch-wide strap goes around the leg above the knee; it buttons to the short tab and the short tab buttons to Part II just above point 36. The second button's exact location is to be fitted off the horse.

The source's long curved edges are represented by short straight sections between measured points. The waist and back darts are marked for sewing but their intake is not folded in the preview. The source does not specify an exact side-seam assembly or a waist finish, so the brief's order below is a proposed construction sequence. The original 37/38-inch worn front and back lengths are not reproduced directly: the point drafts reach 39 inches from their construction origins, which is the length used here. The front and back side lengths may need easing and levelling in muslin. The button's final position is deliberately a fitting decision, as Mitchell directs.

## Pieces and proposed construction

Cut one Part I, one Part II, one long leg strap and one short tab. Sew the marked darts, leave the marked back opening, join the drapes at their corresponding side edges, and finish the waist. Finish the cutaway and hem edges. Attach the long strap at Part I's point 17 and the short tab above point 15. Fit the long strap around the leg just above the knee, button it to the short tab, then fit the tab's button on Part II above point 36 while the wearer stands off the horse. Level the long hem in a muslin before cutting final cloth.
