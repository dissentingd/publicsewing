# Work aprons: brief

**Status: draft.** Not sewn or fit tested.

**Threadloop:** "apron" has 84 free patterns (Tessuti, Purl Soho, Made for Mermaids among them), and FreeSewing's Albert is another. They're mostly kitchen and craft aprons, not Vincent's trade aprons, and nothing listed says it's drafted from measurements.

## Source
Vincent, *The Cutter's Practical Guide to Cutting and Making Shirts, Undergarments* (1890), book p. 39, PDF p. 43, `vincentmakingshirts18xx`. One plate presents eight reduced apron diagrams. The text describes materials, fastening and pocket placement; dimensions are written on the diagrams.

| Diagram | Source shape and dimensions | Our pattern |
|---|---|---|
| 1, plasterer's | Bib, 10 in neck width, about 38 in body width, 30 in below waist, two upper side pockets; neck and waist tapes | Default bib body, two pockets, three ties |
| 2, steward's/carpenter's | Bib, 10 in neck width, about 35 in body width and 33 in below waist; ties | `carpenter` |
| 3, butcher's square | Square top, about 35 × 36 in; ties | `butcherSquare` |
| 4, butcher's pointed | Square body with a point and buttonhole above; about 35 in wide, 38 in below waist | `butcherPoint` |

| 8, painter's | Bib, about 33 in body width and 33 in below waist, large central tool pocket; neck tape and waist ties | `painter` |

## Scaling and construction
The plate's dimensions are fixed inches for adults. We scale the 38 in plasterer's body width from the 36 in adult model's 30½ in waist, the 30 in below-waist length from its 44¾ in waist-to-floor, and the 10 in bib height from the same. Other diagrams use source ratios to those three dimensions. The adjustable width and length controls therefore change all styles, including children's sizes. This scaling is ours; Vincent does not give a graded apron rule.

The shapes are straight-edged, with a standard seam allowance. The plate shows curved shoulders and pocket bottoms; our polygon pieces simplify these to straight lines. The two side pockets for Diagram 1 and central pocket for Diagram 8 are separate cut pieces; position them as shown on the plate. Waist and neck ties are cut on folds. Add the hook, elastic tab button, or fringe where the diagram calls for them. The tie lengths and widths are our sewing choices, because the source does not dimension them.

## Checks
Draft all eight styles at adult, child and stout sizes; compare the default with the plate. Automated checks draft every style at all supported measurement sets, check allowances, show exactly one body and the relevant ties and pockets, and check the pointed and barman button marks.
