# Box overcoat

**Source:** Simons, *Designing Overcoat Patterns for Men and Young Men* (1916), corpus
`designingovercoa00simo` (tracked). Medium Box Overcoat, pp. 14–15; the Extreme Box Overcoat,
pp. 12–13, for comparison. US 1916, public domain. Inches below are Simons', for a 38 breast.

**Block:** FreeSewing's Brian, through the overcoat (`designs/overcoat`): its single-breasted
front, notched lapel and collar, vent, pockets and lining, cut looser and flared to a full sweep.

**Status:** draft. Not sewn.

## What the source shows
- **Medium box:** "finishing up about 66 inches sweep", 44 in long; the breast drafted three
  sizes up; the shoulders narrower than the regular overcoat's and the back neck ⅜ in wider; the
  CB a straight line slanting 1¼ in out to the hem (B–H); the side seam a straight line from
  the armhole flaring to the hem; no waist shaping.
- **Extreme box:** four sizes up, 42 in long, the front 5 in past the half breast (Y–Z).

## How it maps onto the block
| Feature | Simons | Here (option default) |

| Ease | Three sizes up | `chestEase` 27% (the overcoat's 24%, one size more) |
| Length | 44 in | `lengthBelowWaist` 250% of waist to seat, as the overcoat |
| Sweep | About 66 in at the hem | `hemFlare` 10% of the seat at each side seam: 66 in at FreeSewing's 38 |
| Everything else | — | As the overcoat |

## Changes and what isn't modelled
- **The flare starts at the seat line**, as the coat body lets out a side seam, not on a
  straight line from the armhole; the CB stays straight (with the vent) rather than slanting out.
- **Shoulder and neck:** Simons' narrower shoulder and wider back neck aren't modelled.
- **The extreme box** is this design with more ease (`chestEase` about 30%), shorter
  (`lengthBelowWaist` about 230%); not a separate design.

## Construction

1. Make the slanted hand welt pockets and their bags before closing the coat body. Interface the facings, collar pieces, and pocket welts as needed.
2. If breast pockets are selected, prepare them and stitch them at P6 with their flaps.
3. If lower patch pockets are selected, prepare them and stitch them at P8 with their flaps.
4. Sew the centre-back seam and vent, then the A shoulder and B side seams. The box shape comes from the side flare; do not add waist shaping.
5. Sew the G collar outer edge, turn it, and attach it at D to the neckline. Attach the E front facings to the lapel and front edge.
6. Close the F sleeve underarm seams and set the sleeves at C, easing their caps. Make the lining and attach it to the facings.
7. If a belt is selected, prepare it and attach it at BT before finishing the coat hem.
8. Finish the vent and H coat and sleeve hems with room for the lining to move. Work the BH front buttonholes and sew on the buttons. This follows the overcoat and peacoat construction in this collection.
