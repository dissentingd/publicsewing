# Box overcoat

**Source:** Simons, *Designing Overcoat Patterns for Men and Young Men* (1916), corpus
`designingovercoa00simo` (tracked). Medium Box Overcoat, pp. 14–15; the Extreme Box Overcoat,
pp. 12–13, for comparison. US 1916, public domain. Inches below are Simons', for a 38 breast.

**Block:** FreeSewing's Brian, through the overcoat (`designs/overcoat`): its single-breasted
front, notched lapel and collar, vent, pockets and lining, cut looser and flared to a full sweep.

**Status:** draft. Not sewn.

## What the source shows
- **Medium box:** "finishing up about 66 inches sweep", 44 in long; the breast drafted three
  sizes up; the shoulders narrower than the regular overcoat's and the back neck ⅜ in wider; the
  CB a straight line slanting 1¼ in out to the hem (B–H); the side seam a straight line from
  the armhole flaring to the hem; no waist shaping.
- **Extreme box:** four sizes up, 42 in long, the front 5 in past the half breast (Y–Z).

## Changes and what isn't modelled
- **The flare starts at the seat line**, as the coat body lets out a side seam, not on a
  straight line from the armhole; the CB stays straight (with the vent) rather than slanting out.
- **Shoulder and neck:** Simons' narrower shoulder and wider back neck aren't modelled.
- **The extreme box** is this design with more ease (`chestEase` about 30%), shorter
  (`lengthBelowWaist` about 230%); not a separate design.
