# Churidar

**Source:** inspired by Central Board of Secondary Education with the National Institute of Fashion
Technology, *Basic Pattern Development: Students Handbook + Practical Manual, Class XII* (CBSE, Delhi,
2014), the maintainer's copy; not in the repository. In copyright: only its methods and proportions
are used. This text and the pattern are our own.

**Threadloop (2026-11-15):** "churidar" finds no free patterns.

**Block:** none. Cut flat from straight lines, as the salwar (the `polygon` piece type); a hidden Titan
part carries the measurements. The `polygon` piece type gained an optional `grain` line, here at 45°
for the bias; all seventeen designs using polygon pieces snapshot identical. Drafted from the wearer's
seat, waist to floor, knee and heel, so it fits any size.

**Status:** draft. Not sewn.

## What we took
The bias-cut leg, full at the top and close below the knee, its extra length falling into folds at the
ankle, with a gathered belt, at comparable proportions.

## How it maps onto the block
| Feature | Here (option default) |
|---|---|
| Length | `churidarLength` 120% of waist to floor |
| Top | `topWidth` 33.3% of the seat, each half |
| Crotch | `crotchShare` 33.3% of the length below the belt |
| Thigh | `thighCurve` 4% of the seat, drawn as three points |
| Knee | `kneeWidth` 111% of the knee |
| Ankle | `ankleWidth` 108% of the heel |
| Belt | `beltDepth` 19.4%, `casingDepth` 5.6%, `beltWidth` 56.9% of the seat, never wider than the legs' tops |
| Grain | The grainline at 45° to the fold |

## Changes and what isn't modelled
- **The thigh curve** is three straight segments: true it when cutting.
- **The ankle** has no opening, since its width passes over the heel. Many churidars have a short slit
  with hooks or buttons there; this one doesn't.
- **The lay:** place the pattern at 45° to the selvedge, following its grainline.
- **No tradition marker** yet: which to show is the maintainer's choice.

## Pieces
Legs (two, each on the fold, on the bias), belt (one, on the fold).

## Construction

1. Close each leg's I inseam, from the ankle up to the crotch notch.
2. Join the two legs along their X crotch edges, front and back.
3. Close the belt's B ends. Gather the legs' tops into its lower edge at W.
4. Fold the CS casing over and thread a drawstring or elastic.
5. Finish the H ankle hems. When worn, the extra length gathers in folds there.
