# Harrington jacket

**Source:** none. Our own short woven jacket on FreeSewing's Brian block, using the project's raglan sleeve and rib piece. The proportions are our choices, adjustable through options.

**Block:** Brian's body, cut to the upper hip. The raglan operation moves each shoulder section onto one sleeve piece. The body and sleeve edges are recorded for ribbing cut shorter than the edge it finishes.

**Status:** draft. Not sewn.

**Threadloop (2026-11-12):** [Birdie Bomber Jacket](https://threadloop.app/patterns/birdie-bomber-jacket-by-sew-this-pattern) is a raglan zip jacket with rib trim, and [Fashion Freaks' free Men's Raglan Jacket](https://threadloop.app/patterns/mens-raglan-jacket-by-fashion-freaks) is a woven zip alternative. No third-party pattern pieces were used.

## The design

| Element | Default | Option | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body fit | 16% chest ease, 8% seat ease | `chestEase`, `seatEase` | Room for a layer below |
| Length | 65% of waist to seat below waist | `lengthBelowWaist` | Short jacket |
| Sleeve | one-piece raglan, 25% biceps ease | `bicepsEase`, `sleeveLengthBonus` | Ease of movement |
| Neck | standing collar 9% of neck wide | `collarWidth` | Closes to the throat |
| Front | separating zip, 2% chest stand, no drop | `buttonExtension`, `frontDrop` | Straight zip front |
| Ribbing | cuffs 18% of wrist high, waistband 4% of chest high, both 15% shorter than their sewn edges | `cuffHeight`, `bandHeight`, `ribStretch` | Draws in wrist and hem |

## Variants

- **Relaxed:** 24% chest ease and a deeper waistband.

## Changes and what isn't modelled

- The rib trim comes from the actual sleeve and body hem lengths, so it grades with the wearer. The bands are deliberately shorter; stretch them evenly between quarter marks while sewing.
- A classic Harrington often has pockets, a lining and a back yoke. This simplified version has none. It does have a separate front facing, stand collar, separating zip, rib cuffs and waistband.
- The one-piece raglan sleeve has a shoulder dart where its front and back sections meet; sew it before attaching the sleeve.

## Pieces

1. Back, on the fold.
2. Front, cut two mirrored.
3. Front facing, cut two.
4. One-piece raglan sleeve, cut two.
5. Rib cuff, cut two.
6. Rib waistband, cut one, open at the zip.
7. Stand collar, cut two or self-lined.

## Construction

1. Sew the sleeve shoulder darts, then the raglan seams and side and sleeve underarm seams.
2. Join the collar to the neck, then attach the front facings and separating zip.
3. Join the rib cuffs into rings, fold and stretch them onto the sleeve hems.
4. Fold the waistband and sew it across the body hem, stretching between quarter marks. Finish its two ends at the zip.
