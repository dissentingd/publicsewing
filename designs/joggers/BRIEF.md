# Joggers

**Source:** Our own design on FreeSewing's Titan trouser block. The proportions and pocket placement are our choices.

**Block:** Titan front and back, redrawn as relaxed pull-on legs. Each leg's hem edge is recorded to size its rib cuff. The back outseam is trued to the front.

**Status:** draft. Not sewn.

**Threadloop (2026-11-12):** No third-party pattern pieces were used.

## The design

| Element | Default | Option | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fit | 8% seat ease | `seatEase` | Ease for a pull-on garment |
| Length | 95% of waist to floor | `ankleLength` | Room for a rib cuff |
| Taper | 33% of seat removed at the outer hem, with a 20% ankle ease floor | `legTaper`, `ankleEase` | Relaxed upper leg, fitted ankle |
| Waist | Elastic casing, 3.5% of waist | `casing` | Pull-on opening |
| Pockets | In-seam pockets with opening from 25% to 85% of waist to seat | `pockets`, `sidePocketTop`, `sidePocketLength`, `sidePocketDepth` | Accessible side pocket |
| Cuffs | 18% of ankle high and 15% shorter than the sewn hem | `cuffHeight`, `ribStretch` | Draws in the ankle |

## Variants

- **Plain:** the same joggers without pockets.

## Changes and what isn't modelled

- The trouser legs are cut from Titan's woven block. Choose a stable knit or make a muslin before using a stretch knit; negative ease and stretch recovery are not modelled.
- The pocket bag is a separate shaped piece, cut four. The side seam has matching mouth notches on front and back. Finish the pocket bag's free curved edges before joining it to the side seam.
- The rib cuff's cut length uses the actual sewn leg hems. The waist elastic length is chosen during fitting, not printed as a pattern piece.

## Pieces

1. Front leg, cut two mirrored.
2. Back leg, cut two mirrored.
3. Rib cuff, cut two.
4. Pocket bag, cut four if pockets are selected.

## Construction

1. If using pockets, sew a bag to each front and back pocket opening, matching the notches. Join each bag pair around its free edge.
2. Sew front to back at the outseams above and below the pocket mouths. Sew inseams and crotch seam.
3. Turn and stitch the elastic casing, leaving an opening to insert and adjust elastic.
4. Join each rib cuff into a ring, fold it and stretch it evenly onto the corresponding leg hem.
