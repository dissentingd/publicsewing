# Kalidar kurta

**Source:** inspired by Central Board of Secondary Education with the National Institute of Fashion
Technology, *Basic Pattern Development: Students Handbook + Practical Manual, Class XII* (CBSE, Delhi,
2014), the maintainer's copy; not in the repository. In copyright: only its methods and proportions
are used. This text and the pattern are our own.

**Threadloop (2026-11-16):** not searched for this one.

**Block:** none: cut flat from straight-edged `polygon` pieces, as the salwar is, on a hidden Brian
part that only carries the measurements. No library code changed.

**Status:** draft. Not sewn.

## What we took
The panel layout of a kalidar kurta, with a centre panel, tapering side panels (kalis), a straight
sleeve and a square underarm gusset, at comparable proportions.

## How it maps onto the pieces
| Feature | Here (option default) |
|---|---|
| Length | Back neck to waist plus `skirtLength` 50% of waist to floor |
| Centre panel | `panelWidth` 20.9% of the chest |
| Armhole and sleeve width | `armholeDepth` 22.1% of the chest |
| Shoulder drop | `shoulderDrop` 2.2% of the chest |
| Sleeve hem | `sleeveTaper` 5.8% of the chest |
| Sleeve length | `sleeveLength` 79% of shoulder to wrist, from the shoulder point |
| Side panel top and hem | `kaliTop` 9.8%, `kaliHem` 19.5% of the chest |
| Side panel corners | `kaliCurve` 1.4% of the chest |
| Neck | `neckWidth` 8.3%, `neckDepth` 11.2%, `backNeckDepth` 2.9% of the chest |
| Placket | `placketDepth` 16.7% of the length; `placketWidth` 8% of the neck |

## Changes and what isn't modelled
- **The gusset** is sized to the side panels' tops (`kaliTop` with the corner's drop), so its seams
  match.
- **The side panel's length** is the centre panel's side below the sleeve, so it ends level with the
  hem.
- **Every width is a share of the chest,** which keeps the ease consistent at every size.
- **The neck** is a quarter ellipse in straight steps, since polygon pieces have no curves.
- **The placket pieces and three buttons** are our own.
- **No collar** is drawn.
- **The sleeve is never shorter than** the gusset plus 4 cm, so the underarm seam has a length to sew.

## Variants
- **Short kurta:** to the hip (`skirtLength` 20%), the side panels narrower at the hem (`kaliHem` 14%).
- **Long sleeves:** to the wrist (`sleeveLength` 100%).

## Pieces
1. Front panel, on the fold, with the placket slit marked.
2. Back panel, on the fold.
3. Sleeve, on the fold (the top of the arm), cut two.
4. Side panel (kali), cut four: the straight edge sews to a centre panel, the sloping edge to the
   next side panel at the side seam.
5. Underarm gusset, cut two, on the bias.
6. Placket, cut two, each folded in half lengthways.

## Construction
1. Cut the slit; sew a placket to each side of it, the buttonholed one on top; finish the slit's foot.
2. Sew the shoulders; bind or face the neck.
3. Sew each sleeve's top edge to the panels' sides, shoulder seam to the sleeve's fold, down to the
   notches.
4. Sew a side panel to each side of each centre panel, from the notch to the hem, its straight edge to
   the panel.
5. Set in the gusset: two sides to the front and back side panels' tops, two to the sleeve's underarm
   edges up to the notches. Sew the underarm seams from the notch to the hem, and the side seams from
   the gusset to the hem.
6. Hem the kurta and the sleeves; sew on the buttons.
