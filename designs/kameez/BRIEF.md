# Kameez

**Source:** inspired by Central Board of Secondary Education with the National Institute of Fashion
Technology, *Basic Pattern Development: Students Handbook + Practical Manual, Class XII* (CBSE, Delhi,
2014), the maintainer's copy; not in the repository. In copyright: only its methods and proportions
are used. This text and the pattern are our own.

**Threadloop (2026-11-15):** not searched for this one ("salwar" found none).

**Block:** FreeSewing's Brian, through the yoke template and the shift dress (`extends:
../shift-dress/spec.yaml`): front and back each in one piece. `lib/yoke.mjs` gained `sleeveFinish` (a
design option): `hem`, a plain sleeve turned up at the hem with no band. All eleven yoke designs
snapshot identical.

**Status:** draft. Not sewn.

## What we took
The dartless A-line shape flaring from the armhole, the side slits and the short plain sleeve, at
comparable proportions.

## How it maps onto the block
| Feature | Here (option default) |
|---|---|
| Ease | `chestEase` 12% |
| Flare | `hemFlare` 8.3% of the seat |
| Length | `skirtLength` 50% of waist to floor, below the waist |
| Slits | `sideSlit` 50% of the waist to hem |
| Neck | The shift dress's round neck, bound |
| Opening | `opening` front, `placketDepth` 45%, one button or hook |
| Sleeve | `sleeves` short, `shortSleeve` 46% of shoulder to wrist, `sleeveFinish` hem |
| Straight kameez | The `straight` variant: `hemFlare` 4% |

## Changes and what isn't modelled
- **A darted, fitted version** is its own design, `fitted-kameez`.
- **The hem** is straight.
- **The front opening** is our own.
- **Neckline variations** are the shift dress's options: scoop, square, boat and V.
- **No tradition marker** yet: which to show is the maintainer's choice.

## Pieces
Front and back (each on the fold), sleeves, neck placket.

## Construction
1. Join the A shoulders. Bind the D neckline and make the Z front opening.
2. Set in the sleeves at C while the sides are open.
3. Close the F sleeve underarm and B body side seams in one, from the sleeve hem down to the top of each slit.
4. Finish the slits and H lower edge, then the W sleeve hems.
