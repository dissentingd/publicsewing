# Painter's jacket: brief

**Status: draft.** Not sewn or fit tested.

## Source
Vincent, *The Cutter's Practical Guide to Cutting and Making Shirts, Undergarments* (1890), p. 35, PDF p. 39, `vincentmakingshirts18xx`. The text specifies a jacket covering the knees, easy fit, three patch pockets, a single buttoned front with five buttons, a Prussian collar, a narrow front facing, and a sleeve with a button cuff. It calls for the cook's jacket sleeve.

The plate is marked for a 36 in breast. It gives 34 in from neck level to hem and 17½ in to the waist line, with a level hem. The breast pocket is about 5⅝ by 6⅝ in; each lower pocket about 7½ by 8 in. The collar stands about 1¾ in. The plate shows a back, forepart, collar, and cuff detail; the sleeve is carried over from the earlier diagram.

## Our draft
The body, collar, facing and patch pockets extend the existing barber's coat on Brian. The longer body and five-button row follow Vincent. The sleeve has a separate button cuff and placket. Because the plate specifies fixed inches for a single breast size, these defaults scale with body measurements. The inherited pocket outlines and positions are approximate; the lower pockets retain the barber's coat proportions rather than Vincent's exact 7½ by 8 in. The collar is the shared standing collar rather than a new reproduction of the Prussian collar plate. The placket opening and cuff overlap are our construction choices where the source gives no dimensions.

The `plain` variant omits all three pockets. All pieces include seam allowance in the generated pattern.
