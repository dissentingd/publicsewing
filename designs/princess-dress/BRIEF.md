# Princess dress

**Source:** none. Our own design on FreeSewing's Noble princess bodice. The proportions below are our choices and adjustable.

**Block:** Noble splits the darted Bella bodice into four panels per half body: centre and side panels at the front and back. `princessDress` continues each panel from its waist to the skirt hem; no horizontal waist seam remains.

**Status:** draft. Not sewn.

**Threadloop (2026-11-12):** [Mood Sewciety's free Aurora dress](https://threadloop.app/patterns/aurora-dress-mdf299-by-mood-sewciety) is another princess seam dress, with an off-the-shoulder neckline and a different closure. No third-party pattern pieces were used.

## The design

| Element | Default | Option | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Length below waist | 45% of waist to floor | `dressLength` | Near the knee on the preview figure |
| Flare at each princess seam | 5% of seat | `princessFlare` | Opens each panel toward the hem |
| Side flare | 8% of seat | `sideFlare` | Walking room and a balanced skirt |
| Centre back zip | from neck to 60% of waist to seat below waist | `zipLength` | Opens the fitted dress for dressing |

## Variants

- **Armhole princess:** Noble's princess lines begin at the armhole instead of the shoulder; the same skirt continues below.

## Changes and what isn't modelled

- Noble stops at the waist. Each piece now runs to the hem, with equal lower extensions on seams sewn together. The princess lines follow the original bodice then become straight, gently flared lower seams.
- The centre back is seamed for a zip, with its lower end notched. No separate zipper guard, facing, lining or pockets are drafted. Bind the neck and armholes, or make a separate lining.
- The side panels flare more at the outer edge than the princess edge. Check the skirt fall and zip length in a muslin.

## Pieces

1. Back centre panel, cut two, centre back seamed for the zip.
2. Back side panel, cut two.
3. Front centre panel, cut one on the fold.
4. Front side panel, cut two.

## Construction

1. Sew each front side panel to the centre front, matching the princess seam and waist points. Do the same for the back.
2. Sew shoulders and side seams; finish neck and armholes with binding or a lining.
3. Sew centre back below the zip notch and install the zip above it.
4. Hem the dress.
