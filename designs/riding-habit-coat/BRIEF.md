# Riding habit coat

**Source:** Weiler, *The Ladies' Tailor* (1915), `weilerladiestail00weil`, “Riding Habit Coat,” printed pp. 82–83 (PDF pp. 86–87). The adjoining tight fitting coat (printed pp. 80–81) supplies the upper construction. **Status:** draft, not sewn or fit tested.

## Source and plate

- The worked draft uses a 40 in hip. The back width is 1¼ in at the waist and 1¾ in at the hip. The text gives an additional 1 in from the back hip point toward A, then two 5 in positions with B between; the second side piece is marked 2½ in each way from B.
- The front goes in ¾ in at the waist, then 2 in, and the hem steps are 4 and 4½ in. The front lap is 2 in above the waist and 1½ in below. The optional back slit is 1¾ in.
- The plate shows a narrow centre back, six shaped body panels per side and a long flared skirt. The text calls for more cloth over the hip for riding.

## Draft

This extends the double dart jacket block, giving six body panels a side, a shawl collar, two piece sleeves, facing and lining. The back waist seam is positioned 3.125% of the seat circumference from centre back (1¼ / 40), and its hem is flared separately to approach the ½ in increase at the hip. The extra hip room is a 14% seat ease default; length 55% of waist to floor and 10% seat per hem seam are chosen from the plate because Weiler gives no finished lengths or ease values. The front button extension is 5.6% of chest, approximating 2 in on a 36 in chest.

The block's seam shaping and shawl collar are its own interpretation. The source's distinct upper and lower lap widths, curved back slit, and point by point 2½ in side piece construction are not reproduced. Check the paper pattern and make a muslin before cutting cloth.
