# Shell top

**Source:** none. Our own design on FreeSewing's Bella block. All proportions below are our choices, adjustable through options.

**Block:** Bella's darted front and back. `bodiceNeck` shapes the neckline; `bodiceHip` extends each piece past the waist and continues its waist dart to a point below it. The bust dart remains at the side.

**Status:** draft. Not sewn.

**Threadloop (2026-11-12):** [Free shell top search](https://threadloop.app/patterns?q=shell+top&free=only&commit=Search) is a reference for available styles. No third-party pattern pieces were used.

## The design

| Element | Default | Option | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neck | round, widened 5% of neck, lowered 25% of front neck-to-waist | `neckShape`, `neckWiden`, `neckDepth` | An easy shell neckline |
| Back neck | lowered 5% of back neck-to-waist | `backNeckDepth` | Comfortable back edge |
| Length | 85% of waist to seat below the waist | `hipLength` | Hip-length top |
| Flare | 5% of seat added at each side below waist | `hipFlare` | Room over the hip |

## Variants

- **Square neck:** a wider square opening on the same body.

## Changes and what isn't modelled

- The original Bella bodice stops at the waist. These panels continue below it without a waist seam. The lower dart legs meet inside the piece and are sewn as part of each waist dart.
- Neck and armhole facings are not separate pieces; finish with bias binding or a lining. No zip is drafted. Check that the finished opening passes over the wearer's shoulders and bust before cutting final fabric.

## Pieces

1. Front, on the fold.
2. Back, on the fold.

## Construction

1. Sew the front bust dart, then the front and back waist darts through their lower points.
2. Sew shoulder and side seams. Finish neck and armholes with binding or a lining.
3. Hem the top.
