# Skirt coat

**Source:** N. S. Sheifer, *System of Designing and Grading Ladies', Misses' & Children's Garments* (1908), "Drafting a Skirt Coat," text PDF p. 80 and plate PDF p. 81 (`nssheiferssystem00shei`). Public domain.

**Block:** FreeSewing Breanna through the built `girls-coat`, which supplies the box body, neck dart, notched lapel, collar, pockets, two-piece sleeves, and child proportions.

**Threadloop:** A search for skirt and flared coats found similar commercial patterns, including [Erica Coat](https://threadloop.app/patterns/erica-coat-by-eseriel-patterns), but no free equivalent drafted from measurements.

**Status:** Draft, not sewn.

## What the source shows

Sheifer first cuts the coat's plain body parts. The skirt begins about 2 inches below the waist; this placement is a style choice. Its join curves around the body, and the front is about 1 inch lower than the back. The plate shows a broad, flared skirt and a small hip dart at B. The text places the front and side gore 3/4 inch apart about 1 1/2 inches below the waist, then laps them at the hip. The plate does not provide separate full-size cutting dimensions for the skirt.

## How it maps onto the block

The original box coat is divided into upper front/back and lower front/back pieces by one curved line on each half body. Both side cuts start the same distance from their armholes, keeping the bodice side seams matched. The front skirt is pivoted about a point below the join to open the hip dart. Its two top segments keep their original combined length, so after the dart is sewn the skirt join matches the bodice. The buttons stop above the join. The skirt-coat page has no pocket instruction or drawing, so the inherited pockets are off.

| Feature | Source | Draft default |
|---|---|---|
| Back skirt join | About 2 in below waist, by taste | `skirtDrop` 5.6% of chest, 2.02 in at a 36 in chest |
| Front drop | About 1 in lower than back | `frontSkirtDrop` 2.8% of chest, 1.01 in at 36 in |
| Side height and curve | Read from the plate | `skirtSideDrop` 1.4% and `skirtCurve` 0.7% of chest |
| Hip dart intake | 3/4 in separation at B | `hipDartIntake` 2.1% of chest, 0.76 in at 36 in |

| Body, flare, collar, sleeve | Plain coat parts | Inherited from `girls-coat` |

## Changes and what is not modelled

The plate lays several existing gores together to draw one skirt. This draft uses separate front and back skirts sewn at the side for the inherited box body. The dart opens a small wedge in the front skirt, with equal-length dart legs; its depth is a plate estimate. The source's overlapping gore layout is represented by the curved join and dart, not reproduced as a one-piece skirt. The inherited lapel is a design choice from the built base; Sheifer gives no new lapel for this skirt treatment.

## Pieces and construction

Upper front and back, front and back skirt, facing, collar, top and under sleeves. Use the matching curved cut edges when joining the skirts; the front skirt's dart intake is taken out before that seam is sewn.

1. Mark the front skirt dart legs and tip and the curved bodice/skirt joins. Sew the hip darts from the upper edge toward their tips and press them.
2. Sew the inherited upper-front neck darts. Join the upper fronts and back at shoulders, then press the seams.
3. Join each skirt to its matching upper piece along the curved edge, matching centre and side ends. Press the joins; check that the front hem falls below the back as drafted.
4. Sew the continuous bodice and skirt side seams. Assemble each two-piece sleeve and set it into the matching armhole.
5. Apply the inherited facing and collar. Finish sleeve and skirt hems, then work the buttonholes and attach the buttons above the skirt join.
