# Ulster

**Source:** Simons, *Designing Overcoat Patterns for Men and Young Men* (1916), corpus
`designingovercoa00simo` (tracked). D.B. Ulster Collar Overcoat, pp. 28–30. US 1916, public
domain. Inches below are Simons', for a 38 breast.

**Block:** FreeSewing's Brian, through the peacoat (`designs/peacoat`), which already takes this
draft's front, collar, buttons and pockets, at hip length. The ulster is the peacoat at Simons'
full length, drafted looser.

**Status:** draft. Not sewn.

## What the source shows
- **Size:** "drafted 4 sizes larger than the breast measurement of sack": a 38 drafted by the 42.
- **Length:** 50 in from the nape (B–G); the waist 17½ in down (B–E). The nape (A–B) is drawn a
  little higher than the regular draft's (the fraction is unclear in the scan), taken off the
  collar stand.
- **Front, collar, buttons, pockets, back:** as read for the peacoat (its brief): a 7 in stand,
  three buttons a row, a notched lapel with a deep ulster collar that buttons to the throat, a
  slanted welt pocket from the waist to the hip line, a CB seam.
