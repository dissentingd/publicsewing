# Wrap dress

**Source:** none. Our own design on FreeSewing's Bella darted bodice. The proportions are our choices and are adjustable.

**Block:** Bella, reshaped into two crossing surplice fronts. The skirt has two overlapping fronts that meet the bodice at the waist, and a separate back. The ties close the waist.

**Status:** draft. Not sewn.

**Threadloop (2026-11-12):** [Jess Dang's free wrap-dress tutorial](https://threadloop.app/patterns/vintage-wrap-dress-by-jess-dang) and [Untitled Thoughts' free Louise wrap dress](https://threadloop.app/patterns/louise-wrap-dress-by-untitled-thoughts) are existing alternatives. No third-party pattern pieces were used.

## The design

| Element | Default | Option | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Back neck | widened 5% of neck, lowered 5% of back neck-to-waist | `neckWiden`, `backNeckDepth` | Follows the shoulder of the front |
| Crossing fronts | each reaches 25% of waist past centre front | `wrapOverlap` | Coverage when tied |
| Sleeves | Bella's fitted sleeve | `sleeves` | Can be omitted for a bound armhole |
| Skirt | A-line, 50% of waist-to-floor, 6% seat ease, 8% seat flare at the side | `skirtLength`, `seatEase`, `hemFlare` | Knee area length and walking room |
| Ties | 120% of waist long, 3% of waist-to-seat wide | `tieLength`, `tieWidth` | Enough length to wrap and tie |

## Variants

- **Sleeveless:** omit the sleeve and bind the armhole.

## Changes and what isn't modelled

- Bella's centre front is replaced with a long diagonal from the shoulder to the far side of the waist. Cut two mirrored fronts.
- The waist darts stay in the bodice and skirt; the skirt uses two overlapping front panels. No zipper is needed.
- The pattern does not mark a side-seam pass-through for the inner tie. Make a reinforced opening in one side seam at the waist, sized to the finished tie. Secure the inner tie to the inner front and the outer tie to the outer front.
- Bind or face both diagonal neck edges. A facing is not separately drafted. A muslin must check front coverage and neck gaping before final fabric.

## Pieces

1. Front bodice, cut two mirrored.
2. Back bodice, cut two.
3. Front skirt, cut two mirrored.
4. Back skirt, cut two.
5. Tie, cut two.
6. Sleeve, cut two when selected.

## Construction

1. Sew the bust, waist and skirt darts.
2. Sew shoulder seams and finish the front diagonal and back neckline.
3. Join each front skirt to its front bodice; join back skirt to back bodice.
4. Sew one side seam with a reinforced opening at the waist for the inner tie, then the other side seam. Attach ties to the two front waist tips.
5. Set sleeves if selected, otherwise bind armholes. Finish the overlapping skirt fronts and hem.
