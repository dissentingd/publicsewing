# PublicSewing

Made-to-measure sewing patterns for practical, timeless garments, drafted from your own
measurements, children's sizes included.

Each design takes its pieces, style lines, proportions and construction from a public-domain
drafting book (so far five, published 1909 to 1921). The geometry comes from
[FreeSewing](https://freesewing.org)'s tested blocks, so every design drafts at any size. The
old books are design references: designs are modernised and simplified freely.

PublicSewing is a separate project built on FreeSewing's MIT-licensed packages. It is not part
of FreeSewing and isn't endorsed by it.

:::warning[Every design is a draft]
None has been sewn yet. Each one drafts cleanly at every measurement set in the test suite, and
its seams that sew together have matching lengths. That doesn't prove the garment fits. A
design is marked *tested* only after someone has sewn a muslin and reported it.
:::

## Testers wanted

If you're comfortable with pattern terms and willing to cut a muslin from an untested draft:

1. Pick a [design](/designs) and [measure yourself](/measure) (or use one of the size sets).
2. Export a tiled PDF from the design's page and [print and sew a muslin](/muslin).
3. [Report what happened](/report). A report moves the design towards *tested*.

## Privacy

No accounts, no cookies, no analytics. Your measurements stay in your browser's local storage;
nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.
