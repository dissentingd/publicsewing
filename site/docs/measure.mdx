The patterns draft from body measurements, not garment sizes: the ease is in the design. Measure
over light underwear, standing relaxed, with a helper if you can.

- Use a soft tape. Keep it level and snug, not tight.
- Tie a string round your natural waist first and leave it there: several lengths start from it.
- Enter measurements in the pattern tool under **Measurements** (cm by default; switch to inches
  under *Core settings*). They're kept in this browser and offered to every design as
  **My measurements**.
- Not ready to measure? Pick a size set instead: FreeSewing's adult sets, three stout figures,
  or the 75 children's sets from the USDA's 1939 survey of American children (B, C and D are a
  slimmer, average and fuller hip at each height).
