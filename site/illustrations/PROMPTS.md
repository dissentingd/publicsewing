# Illustration prompts

One finished-garment illustration per design, for the top of its page and the Designs table.
Each prompt describes the design **at its default options**, which is what the pattern drafts
before anyone changes anything. Written from each design's `spec.yaml` and `BRIEF.md`
(2026-10-27; the ЕМКО designs added the same day). When a design's defaults change, update its
entry here.

Save each image as `site/illustrations/<design>.jpg` (square, at least 1024 px).

**To make next: the last section of this file**, which holds every prompt still waiting for an image.

## Shared style (prepend to every prompt)

> A single garment drawn as a vintage technical fashion illustration: ink line drawing with
> soft watercolour shading, on aged cream drafting paper with faint pencil construction lines
> and crop marks. Three-quarter front view, the garment on an invisible form (no person, no
> head, no hands, no hanger), centred, the whole garment in frame with margin all round. No
> text, no labels, no logo, no background objects. Square format.

**Every prompt:** the garment exactly as described, with nothing added. Image models tend to add
cuffs, tabs, epaulettes, extra pockets and modern hardware: the "Leave out" lines name the usual
ones. Men's garments button left over right, women's right over left. "Wearer's left" is on the
viewer's right.

>
> Leave out: sleeves, lapels, a V-neck, back belt or buckle, colour.
